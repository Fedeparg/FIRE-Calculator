---
title: "Donations, an About page and search-engine presence"
---

Anyone who wants to support the project can buy a coffee by card from the home page or the new About page. Every page also declares its canonical address, its counterpart in the other language and the preview shown when it is shared on social networks, and the findings of a code audit are applied.

- Applies the findings of a code audit: security headers in the browser, updated libraries and a test suite for the server.
- Every page declares its canonical address, its version in the other language and its preview when shared, and the site publishes a map for search engines.
- Card donations through Stripe, an About page and credits in the footer.
