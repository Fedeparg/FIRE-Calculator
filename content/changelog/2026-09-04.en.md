---
title: "A more complete portfolio history"
---

- Restores the portfolio chart axis fit, which had been lost while merging changes.
- The portfolio chart axis fits the actual range of values, so changes are visible even with a large net worth.
- Each position shows the date of the price it is valued at.
- Stretches of the portfolio chart computed from estimated prices are clearly marked.
- When you add a new holding, the portfolio rebuilds its last year of history instead of starting from scratch.
