---
title: "Sextante goes live"
highlight: true
---

Sextante starts running at sextante.fpardo.net, with encrypted database backups and real sign-in emails. The same day brings the connection for AI assistants: anyone who authorises it can ask Claude or ChatGPT to read or change their portfolio, with separate permissions for reading and writing and the option to revoke access at any time. The portfolio gains an aggregate total, gain or loss per position, a choice of ten currencies and daily closing prices.

- The AI-connection block on the home page stops repeating the same button.
- Rewritten home page, with the connection for AI assistants front and centre, and a logo in the emails.
- The portfolio table no longer forces you to scroll sideways, and refreshes itself when you come back to the tab.
- AI assistants connect to the portfolio without hiccups.
- Connection for AI assistants: Claude or ChatGPT can read and change your portfolio with your permission, and access can be revoked from your account.
- Encrypted database backups, stored away from the server.
- First production deployment of Sextante, published automatically from the repository.
- You can download all your data and delete your account from the settings, as the GDPR requires.
- The email with the sign-in link is now actually sent, in both plain-text and formatted versions.
- Recognises an instrument's ISIN or ticker and maps it to the symbol its quote is looked up with.
- Portfolio total added up in a single currency, converting each position at the day's rate.
- A gain-or-loss column for each position and a ten-currency selector.
- Daily closing prices for each instrument, stored on our own server and updated every night.
- A session whose account no longer exists stops granting access, and editing a position no longer lets you clear the broker it already had.
- Specific error messages when adding or editing a position, and a clear notice when the session has expired.
