---
title: "Compound interest fixed and lighter pages"
---

We fixed a compound interest bug: until now, how often you contributed also decided how often interest was compounded, so with a contribution of 0 the result changed when switching from monthly to annual. These are now two separate things: you choose on its own how often interest is credited. Each page also loads only the text it needs, so it is noticeably lighter. We also fixed a bug where a Trade Republic import could link a stock to a different product sharing its ticker.

- Compound interest: new "Interest compounding" field, independent from the contribution frequency (annual by default). With a contribution of 0, the result no longer depends on how often you contribute.
- FIRE, retirement and the portfolio goal read the return as an effective annual one; with monthly contributions, figures come out somewhat lower than before, when they were inflated.
- Credit card: the interest rate is labelled as TIN (nominal rate), which is what the calculation uses.
- Portfolio: on import, some stocks (such as Amazon) were valued with the price of a different product sharing the ticker on another exchange. Now only an exchange where that security is actually listed is used. If you were affected, delete the position and import the CSV again to rebuild its history.
