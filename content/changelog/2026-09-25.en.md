highlight: true
---

A new calculator that simulates 5,000 market scenarios to tell you how likely you are to reach financial independence and, above all, whether your money lasts through retirement. Instead of a single line with a fixed return, you get a fan of good and bad scenarios and when you get there in each. The project's libraries are also brought up to date.

- The simulator recalculates without blocking the fields, so typing stays smooth.
- New FIRE Monte Carlo simulator: the probability of reaching financial independence and of your money lasting through retirement, with a fan of 5,000 scenarios and its own explainer in the wiki.
- Charts can draw bands of scenarios as well as lines and areas.
- The simulation engine is reproducible: the same inputs always give the same result, including when opening a shared link.
