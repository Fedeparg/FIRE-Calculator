---
title: "Decimals on mobile and a properly drawn sextant"
---

The mobile number keypad now accepts a comma, so typing amounts with cents stops being a fight. The sextant in the logo is also corrected so its arc curves like the real instrument, and it gets its own browser-tab icon.

- Lets you type decimals with a comma on the mobile number keypad.
- Fixes the curve of the sextant's arc in the logo and introduces an icon of the instrument in the browser tab.
