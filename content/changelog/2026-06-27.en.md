---
title: "Accounts with link sign-in, and the first portfolio"
---

The account side of Sextante is built, still before launch: you sign in with a link sent to your email, with no password to remember, and each person can add, edit and delete the positions in their portfolio. The privacy and cookie policy in both languages and the brand home page are written too.

- The broker is only required if you already hold another position in the same instrument.
- Edit positions you already created, with a warning when an identical one exists.
- Add, view and delete the positions in your portfolio.
- Privacy and cookie policy, in Spanish and English.
- Sign-in screen with an email link, and pages reserved for those who are signed in.
- Password-free sign-in through a single-use link that expires after fifteen minutes.
- Fills tax gaps for the self-employed, gift tax and pension plans, and introduces the Sextante brand home page.
