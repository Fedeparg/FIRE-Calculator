The biggest release so far. New capital gains report: your sales matched FIFO across all your brokers, netted by tax year, with an estimated tax bill and a CSV for your return. Portfolio prices refresh every hour on weekdays, and you can ask for an email when your portfolio crosses 25, 50, 75 and 100% of your goal. The goal now tells you how likely you are to reach it, and the Monte Carlo simulator can use over 150 real years of US stocks and bonds. Learn has a portfolio guide and two new articles explaining it all.

- Links inside English articles no longer take you to the Spanish version.
- New portfolio guide in Learn: what you can do and where everything is, linked from the portfolio itself.
- New article on Monte Carlo simulations: how to read a probability of success and when to use the historical model.
- New article on capital gains when selling: FIFO across brokers, offsetting, the two-month rule and currencies.
- Optional email notices when your portfolio crosses 25, 50, 75 and 100% of your FIRE goal, with one-click unsubscribe.
- The portfolio shows when prices were last updated and picks up new ones without reloading the page.
- Portfolio prices refresh every hour from 9:00 to 21:00 on weekdays, not just once a day.
- A recorded sale with no units to sell no longer counts its fees as a loss.
- New portfolio capital gains report: sales by tax year matched FIFO, gains and losses netted, estimated savings tax and CSV download.
- Manually editing the quantity or average price of a position with sales no longer wipes its sale history.
- The Monte Carlo simulator can use real years of US stocks and bonds since 1871, in 10-year stretches, with your chosen share in stocks.
- The simulator shows a table with the probability of success for withdrawal rates from 3% to 5%.
- The portfolio goal shows your probability of success with a Monte Carlo simulation on your real wealth, with a link to the simulator pre-filled with the same data.
