---
title: "Instrument search and prices in the right currency"
---

When adding a position you can search for the instrument and keep its exact symbol, instead of trusting the system to guess: typing a bare 'BTC' could end up valuing the portfolio with another product's price. Positions whose cost is in one currency and whose price is quoted in another are now converted at the day's rate instead of being left unvalued.

- Values positions whose cost is in a different currency from their price, converting at the day's rate.
- Adding an instrument by its ISIN no longer leaves the page waiting, and an overly long name no longer prevents saving the position.
- Instrument search when adding a position, so you keep the exact symbol and see its price straight away.
