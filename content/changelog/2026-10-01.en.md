---
title: "Milestone: your AI assistant can now use all of Sextante, a redesigned portfolio and Trade Republic import"
highlight: true
---

Until today, an assistant connected over MCP (Claude, ChatGPT…) could only see and edit your portfolio. Now it can use all of Sextante: the 27 calculators (mortgages, net salary and income tax by region, FIRE and Monte Carlo, wealth and gift tax…), your realised gains for each tax year to prepare your return, your portfolio breakdown and its progress towards your FIRE goal, and the scenarios you saved. It runs exactly the same code as the website: we moved the calculators and the tax engine into a shared core, so the assistant and the page can never give different numbers. The portfolio also has a new design: instead of stacking everything on one page, it is split into tabs (Summary, Positions, Capital gains and FIRE goal). The Summary tells you at a glance how you are doing; in Positions you search and filter, and each position's detail opens in a panel next to its row, or from the bottom on mobile. You can now bring your Trade Republic buys and sells from its export file, with a preview and no duplicates when you import again; positions sold in full and derivatives each have their own filter, and derivatives do not add to the totals (a new Learn article explains why). And we started measuring which pages are used with Umami on our own server: no cookies, no third parties and none of the values you type into the calculators. More portfolio improvements landed in the afternoon: a second kind of goal (“I want X in N years”), history going back up to 5 years rebuilt from your trades and reaching today, today’s gain in Positions, and a full pass so everything looks right on phones.

- Signing in or out updates the header straight away, without reloading the page.
- Your AI assistant now uses the calculators through two tools: one to discover them and their inputs, and one to calculate.
- The history only shades days before you started tracking your portfolio in Sextante, even if you later import past trades.
- The history chart now reaches today with your portfolio’s current value, without waiting for the nightly snapshot.
- On phones, the history chart uses the full width.
- When you record or import past trades, history is rebuilt for days already captured too, so the invested line no longer drops for no reason.
- The account menu no longer opens off screen on narrow phones.
- On phones, the history picks its range from a dropdown and shows the value and change above instead of an axis.
- On iPhone, portfolio boxes no longer spill off the screen, and the header and tabs fit better.
- On phones the header takes less room, help buttons and links are easier to tap and chart axis figures no longer wrap.
- The Trade Republic import preview shows the resulting average price, computed from each trade’s actual price, so you can check it before confirming.
- The history accounts for stock splits and shades each estimated stretch separately.
- The goal has a second mode, “I want X in N years”: it shows what is left, the contribution needed to get there on time and whether you get there at your current pace.
- Your AI assistant and the email alerts also understand an amount-by-deadline goal.
- Portfolio history now goes back up to 5 years, from your first trade and with what you actually held each day, instead of just the last week.
- The history block is simpler: a short title, compact ranges, the period change on one line and a clear legend for the estimated area.
- The Summary composition expands to show every group, with the same weights as the Positions list.
- Positions can show today's gain as well as the total gain, also in each position's details.
- The FIRE goal has an active plan: the Goal tab loads it when you open it, choosing another makes it active and the Summary says which plan it shows.
- The portfolio Summary is redesigned: the current value up front, this year's gain, the FIRE goal at a glance, the composition as a bar and the positions that moved the most in the last session.
- The Positions tab is redesigned: a search and an open/closed/derivatives filter, four columns and the detail in a panel next to the row (a bottom sheet on mobile).
- My account and Log out move into a user menu in the header.
- The portfolio is split into tabs (Summary, Positions, Capital gains and FIRE goal) instead of stacking everything on one page.
- Newly imported positions show "Looking up price…" and the portfolio refreshes by itself until the quote arrives.
- Trade Republic bonus issues are imported as zero-cost shares, and cash withdrawals no longer show up as unknown transactions.
- More ETFs and non-European stocks (such as Hong Kong ones) now find their quote from the ISIN, so they count towards the portfolio value.
- Your AI assistant can use every calculator and analyse your portfolio: realised gains by tax year, breakdown, progress towards your FIRE goal and saved scenarios.
- New Learn article on derivatives: what they are, their risk and how they are taxed.
- Positions sold in full are hidden (with a checkbox to show them) and derivatives go to a separate section that does not add to the totals.
- Import your buys and sells from the Trade Republic transaction export, with a preview and no duplicates when you import again.
- First-party usage statistics with no cookies or third parties: calculator values are never collected and Do Not Track is honoured.
- Compound interest: interest compounding is now chosen separately from the contribution frequency. With a contribution of 0 the result no longer changes when you change the contribution frequency, and projections (FIRE, retirement, goal) read the return as an effective annual one. With default values, compound interest and retirement now show somewhat lower figures.
