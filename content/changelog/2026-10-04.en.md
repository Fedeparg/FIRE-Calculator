---
title: "Sextante development ends and the code is released"
highlight: true
---

Sextante is discontinued: I will no longer develop it, so I can focus on other projects. Thank you to everyone who used it, tested it and sent feedback over these months. So that what was built stays useful, I am releasing all of its code under the MIT licence: the calculators, the Spanish tax engine, the portfolio and the MCP server are available to anyone who wants to study, reuse or continue them.

- From today there will be no new features or fixes.
- The full source code is published at [github.com/Fedeparg/FIRE-Calculator](https://github.com/Fedeparg/FIRE-Calculator) under the MIT licence.
