The portfolio's capital gains tab is now called **Tax return** and prepares what a foreign broker does not do for you: the savings-base figures for each tax year, converted to euros and shown next to the box of the Spanish return (modelo 100) where they go. Every number carries its provenance (broker data, derived, market, estimate or entered by you), so you know which ones you can copy as they are and which ones are worth checking. It is not advice: it helps you reconcile your return using cited official criteria.

- Foreign-currency sales converted at the ECB reference rate of each trade date, with the exchange difference shown separately, as the Spanish tax authority (DGT) prescribes.
- Two-month rule (shares, funds and derivatives): losses that cannot be claimed yet are deferred and counted when you sell the shares you bought back.
- Dividends and interest: imported from the Trade Republic CSV, or added by hand. Income your broker already reported to the tax agency is flagged.
- Full savings base: the 25 % cross-offset between investment income and gains, and negative balances from the previous four years carried forward automatically.
- International double-taxation credit, capped by the treaty with each country.
- 2025 return boxes next to every figure, with a copy button. CSV export of sales and income, and a print-friendly version.
- New wiki article: how to declare a foreign broker.
- Over MCP, your assistant can read a year's report and manage dividends and interest.
