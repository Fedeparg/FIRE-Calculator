The portfolio stops being a single day’s snapshot: it records every purchase and sale, charts how your net worth and its composition evolve, estimates the tax on a sale by matching lots oldest-first as the law requires, and now shows your progress towards financial independence against the money you actually hold. The income-tax calculators let you pick your autonomous community, any calculation can be shared as a link or saved by name, and this very What’s new page is born today.

- A filtered view of the release notes can be shared and reloaded: the filters travel in the page address.
- The portfolio flags a position valued with a price older than the rest, such as a fund with a delayed net asset value.
- The portfolio page can no longer be dragged sideways over empty background, and four number and date details are corrected.
- The portfolio shows your progress towards financial independence using your real net worth, and can be downloaded as a CSV.
- A new What’s new page with everything that has shipped, release by release.
- Calculators keep their values in the page address, so a calculation can be shared or returned to, and you can save named scenarios to your account.
- Net-worth evolution chart, portfolio composition, purchase detail for each position and a sale simulator that estimates the tax on the gain.
- The portfolio records each purchase and sale with its date and notes its value every day, which is what makes evolution charts and gain calculations possible.
- Income tax is calculated with each autonomous community's own rates in the income calculators.
- The sitemap only reports genuine revision dates and social preview images are served from cache.
- Caps how many calls in a row a connected assistant can make, deletes expired data automatically, and the server stops running with administrator privileges.
- Accessibility: every field has a name, help bubbles open with the keyboard and on touch, and charts expose their data as text for screen readers.
- The website stops running with administrator privileges on the server.
- Every backup is checked to be restorable before it is stored off the server.
- Updates the project's libraries, including several with published security advisories.
