---
title: "How the dividends calculator works"
---

A **dividend** is the share of profit a company distributes to shareholders. When you receive it in Spain, the tax office applies a **withholding** (19 % by default) prepaying the tax on that savings income.

**How to read the result.** The *gross dividend* is shares × dividend per share. The *net* subtracts the withholding. The *dividend yield* relates it to what you invested. If you enter an **annual growth** rate and a horizon, the calculator projects the stream and accumulates the net dividends year by year.

**Worked example** (default values). 100 shares paying €1.50 each, at a price of €50, with 5 % annual dividend growth over 10 years:

- Gross (year 1): 100 × €1.50 = **€150**.
- Withholding (19 %): **€28.50** → net (year 1) **€121.50**.
- Gross dividend yield: €150 / €5,000 = **3 %** (net **2.43 %**).
- After 10 years growing at 5 %: net dividend in the final year **€188.49** and cumulative net dividends **€1,528.21**.

**An important note.** The withholding is a prepayment, not the final tax: dividends are taxed in the savings base (19 %–30 % in brackets). A high dividend doesn't guarantee a good investment, nor does past growth assure the future.
