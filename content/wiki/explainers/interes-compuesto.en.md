---
title: "How the compound interest calculator works"
---

This calculator projects how much your investment could be worth by combining three forces: the **initial capital**, the **regular contributions** and the **annual return** reinvested year after year.

**How to read the result.** The *final value* splits into what you put in (*total contributed*) and what the money generated on its own (*interest earned*). Over the long run, that second part usually ends up the larger.

**Worked example** (default values). You start with €5,000, add €300 a month at 7 % annually for 25 years:

- Total contributed: €5,000 + (€300 × 12 × 25) = **€95,000**.
- Estimated final value: about **€271,649**.
- Interest earned: about **€176,649**, nearly double what you contributed.

**An important note.** The return is an **estimate**, not a guarantee. To think in real purchasing power, subtract inflation (7 % nominal − 3 % ≈ 4 % real).
