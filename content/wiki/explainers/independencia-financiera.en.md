---
title: "How the financial independence calculator works"
---

This calculator answers two FIRE questions: **how much wealth you need** to live off your assets (your *FIRE number*) and **in how many years** you could reach it given your saving and return.

**Where the FIRE number comes from.** Divide your annual spending by the *safe withdrawal rate*. With the classic 4 % rule, that's your spending times 25.

**Worked example** (default values). You want to spend €24,000 a year, already have €20,000 invested, add €800/month and assume a 5 % real return, with a 4 % withdrawal rate:

- FIRE number: €24,000 ÷ 0.04 = **€600,000**.
- Starting from €20,000 and adding €9,600/year at 5 %, you'd take **about 27 years**.

Lower the withdrawal rate to 3.5 % and the target rises (more prudent); raise it and it falls (more risk of depleting the portfolio).

**Bear in mind.** Use a **real** return (net of inflation). When you sell to withdraw, the gain is taxed as a capital gain in the savings base.
