---
title: "How the wealth tax calculator works"
---

This tax falls on your **net wealth** (assets minus debts) above an **exempt minimum**. Only the excess is taxed, and progressively.

**How to read the result.** The *taxable base* is what's left after subtracting your primary residence (exempt up to €300,000) and the exempt minimum. The *tax due* is what you'd pay after your region's rebate.

**Worked example** (default values). Wealth of €1,500,000, primary residence of €300,000, exempt minimum of €700,000:

- Taxable base: 1,500,000 − 300,000 − 700,000 = **€500,000**.
- State tax: about **€1,664** (before any rebate).

**An important note.** Many regions rebate the bill almost to 100 % (Madrid, for instance) or apply their own scale. Adjust the exempt minimum and your region's rebate. It's indicative and not tax advice.
