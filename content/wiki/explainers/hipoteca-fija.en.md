---
title: "How the fixed-rate mortgage calculator works"
---

This calculator uses the **French amortization** system (the standard in Spain): from the principal, the rate and the term it derives your **constant monthly payment**, the **total interest** and the amortization schedule. It also estimates the **APR** including the opening fee and the **linked insurance** (home, life) the bank requires to grant the rate: that's the honest figure for comparing offers.

**How the payment is computed.** With principal *C*, monthly rate *i* (annual rate ÷ 12) and *n* months: Payment = C × i / (1 − (1 + i) ^ −n).

**Worked example** (default values). €180,000 at 3 % nominal over 30 years, with a 0.5 % opening fee and €300/year of linked insurance:

- Monthly payment: **€758.89**.
- Total interest: about **€93,199**.
- Total in payments: **€273,199** (principal + interest).
- Opening fee: **€900**; insurance (30 years): **€9,000**.
- Total cost with fees: **€283,099**.
- **Estimated APR: 3.35 %** (above the 3 % nominal because of the fee and insurance).

**What the chart reveals.** Although the payment is constant, in the early years it's almost all interest; that's why **early repayment** saves far more sooner than later.

**An important nuance.** The nominal rate sets the payment but excludes fees and insurance. To compare banks, look at the **APR**: with the fee and insurance at 0, the APR equals the monthly compounding of the nominal rate (3.04 %); any linked cost pushes it up.
