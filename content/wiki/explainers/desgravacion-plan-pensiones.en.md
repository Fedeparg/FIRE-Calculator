---
title: "How the pension plan tax relief calculator works"
---

Contributing to a pension plan **lowers the base** on which you pay income tax. The saving isn't the whole contribution but the contribution times your **marginal rate**: the more you earn, the more each euro saves you.

**How to read the result.** The *tax saving* is what you avoid paying this year. The *real cost* is what the contribution actually costs you.

**Worked example** (default values). You earn €40,000 and contribute €1,500 (the maximum with relief):

- Tax saving: **€464**.
- Real cost: 1,500 − 464 = **€1,036** (you recover **31 %**).

**An important note.** Only up to €1,500 gets relief (or 30 % of your earned income). When you withdraw the plan you'll be taxed on it as earned income. It's indicative and not tax advice.
