---
title: "How the financial health test works"
---

This test sums up the state of your finances in a **score from 0 to 100** based on eight questions: emergency fund, savings rate, debt, housing cost, investing, retirement planning, protection against setbacks (insurance) and tracking your spending.

**How to read the result.** Each question is weighted by importance (the emergency fund and savings weigh most). The score places you in a category, from *critical* to *strong*, with a priority tip.

**How to use it.** It's not an exam but a starting point. If your score is low, start with what weighs most (an emergency buffer and cutting expensive debt) and retake it in a few months.

**An important note.** It's general guidance based on common personal-finance rules, not a diagnosis tailored to your situation.
