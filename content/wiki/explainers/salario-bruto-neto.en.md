---
title: "How the gross-to-net salary calculator works"
---

Your **gross** salary isn't what you take home: from it you subtract **Social Security contributions** and **income-tax withholding**. This calculator models both in detail, including your **personal and family situation** (age, children, ascendants, disability, joint taxation), which lower income tax through the *personal and family minimum*.

**How to read the result.** The *net per payment* is what you receive each month (or payment). The *personal and family minimum* is the part of income that isn't taxed; the larger your family burden, the lower the tax.

**Worked example** (default values). €30,000 gross, 14 payments, single, age 30, no children, open-ended contract:

- Social Security: €30,000 × 6.5 % = **€1,950**.
- Estimated income tax: **€4,926** (16.4 % withholding).
- Net: **€23,124**, i.e. about **€1,652 per payment**.

Add children or a pension contribution and watch the tax fall.

**An important note.** It applies the state + supplementary regional scale; your region may change the result somewhat. It's indicative and not tax advice.
