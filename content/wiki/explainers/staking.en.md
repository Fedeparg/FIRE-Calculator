---
title: "How the staking calculator works"
---

**Staking** means locking up crypto to help validate a network and, in return, earning rewards. This calculator projects how your position would grow by compounding those rewards at the stated **APY**.

**How to read the result.** The *gross final value* compounds the APY year after year. A **withholding** (19 % by default) is applied to the rewards: the *net final value* subtracts that tax.

**Worked example** (default values). €5,000 staked at 8 % APY for 5 years, with a 19 % withholding on the rewards:

- Gross final value: €5,000 × 1.08⁵ ≈ **€7,346.64**.
- Gross rewards: about **€2,346.64**.
- Withholding (19 %): **€445.86** → net rewards **€1,900.78**.
- Net final value: **€6,900.78**.

**An important note.** Crypto APY is **neither fixed nor guaranteed**, and the asset's price can fall far more than you earn in rewards. It's among the riskiest products. Rewards are taxed as savings income. Not investment advice.
