---
title: "How the budget calculator (50/30/20) works"
---

The **50/30/20** rule splits your net income: **50 %** to needs, **30 %** to wants and **20 %** to saving and investing.

**How to read the result.** The *monthly saving* is what's left after needs and wants. The *savings rate* is that saving over your income: the metric that most drives financial independence. The table compares your actual split with the 50/30/20 target.

**Worked example** (default values). You earn €2,000 net, spend €1,000 on needs and €600 on wants:

- Saving: 2,000 − 1,000 − 600 = **€400/month**, that is **€4,800/year**.
- Your split: **50 %** needs, **30 %** wants, **20 %** savings: right on the rule's target.

**An important note.** 50/30/20 is a guide: in expensive cities needs exceed 50 %. What matters is a positive, growing savings rate.
