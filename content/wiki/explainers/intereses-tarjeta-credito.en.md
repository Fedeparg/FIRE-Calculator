---
title: "How the credit card interest calculator works"
---

**Revolving** credit (paying your card "in instalments" or with a monthly minimum) is among the most expensive debt there is: with APRs above **20 %**, interest piles up fast.

**How to read the result.** You'll see how much you'll pay in **total interest** and how long it'll take to clear the debt. You can choose between two payment modes:

- **Fixed payment**: you pay the same amount every month.
- **Minimum payment (% of balance)**: the typical revolving mode. You pay a percentage of the balance (with a floor in euros); as the balance falls so does the payment, so the debt drags on for far longer.

**Worked example** (default values). A €2,000 debt at 22 % with a fixed payment of €100/month:

- It'd take about **26 months** to clear.
- You'd pay about **€514** in interest alone (total **€2,514**).

**The minimum-payment trap.** That same €2,000 debt at 22 %, but paying the **minimum payment** of 3 % of the balance (with a €25 floor):

- The first payment would be €60 and it'd take about **127 months** (over 10 years).
- You'd pay about **€2,300** in interest: more than the debt itself! (total ~**€4,300**).

**An important note.** Paying off revolving debt is probably the best "investment" available: no safe investment earns what that debt costs. Turn off deferred payment and pay the balance in full each month.
